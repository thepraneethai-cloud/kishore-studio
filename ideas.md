### Idea 1 — "Sacred Manuscript" (Probability: 0.07)

**Design Movement:** Neo-Classical Indian Manuscript meets modern editorial

**Core Principles:**
1. Parchment-warm surfaces with aged texture — every panel feels like a sacred folio
2. Asymmetric two-column layout: narrow left sidebar for workflow steps, wide right canvas for content
3. Ink-drawn iconography for deities — no photographs, only stylized line art
4. Generous breathing room between sections — silence as reverence

**Color Philosophy:** Deep saffron (#C8570A) as primary action color evoking sacred fire; warm cream (#F5EDD6) backgrounds; deep indigo (#1A1040) for text — the palette of ancient palm-leaf manuscripts

**Layout Paradigm:** Left-rail step navigator (fixed, vertical) + scrollable right canvas. No centered hero. Each workflow step is a "folio page" that slides in from the right.

**Signature Elements:**
- Kolam-inspired geometric border motifs as section dividers
- Aged paper grain texture on card surfaces
- Sanskrit/Telugu script as decorative watermarks behind content

**Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate, meditative — no snappy animations. Transitions feel like turning a page. Hover states use warm glow rather than color shifts.

**Animation:** Slow fade-in (600ms ease-out) for page transitions. Cards lift with subtle drop shadow on hover. Step completion triggers a soft "diya flame" particle burst.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Noto Serif Telugu" for headings — authentic, cultural
- Body: "Lora" (serif) for English text — warm, readable
- Accent: "Cinzel Decorative" for section labels

### Idea 2 — "Digital Sanctum" (Probability: 0.06) ← SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Dark Devotional — Sacred Space meets modern creative studio dashboard

**Core Principles:**
1. Deep dark background (near-black with warm undertone) — like the interior of a temple at night
2. Gold and amber as the only accent colors — representing lamp light and divine radiance
3. Persistent left sidebar for workflow navigation; main area is a structured workspace
4. Each tool module is a "shrine panel" — contained, purposeful, glowing

**Color Philosophy:** Background: deep charcoal-brown (#1C1410); Primary accent: warm gold (#D4A017); Secondary: amber (#E8820C); Text: warm off-white (#F0E6D3) — the palette of a lit sanctum at dusk

**Layout Paradigm:** Fixed left sidebar (workflow steps 1–11 as nav items) + main workspace area. No top hero. Workspace uses a two-panel split: input on left, live preview/output on right.

**Signature Elements:**
- Subtle lotus mandala SVG as background watermark
- Gold gradient dividers between sections
- Glowing amber border on active/focused inputs

**Interaction Philosophy:** Focused and productive — this is a creative tool. Interactions are crisp but warm. Completed steps get a golden checkmark glow.

**Animation:** Sidebar items slide-highlight on hover (200ms). Panel transitions use a cross-fade (300ms). Copy-to-clipboard triggers a brief golden ripple.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Cinzel" for sidebar labels and step titles — classical, authoritative
- Body: "Source Sans 3" for content — clean, readable at small sizes
- Telugu text: "Noto Sans Telugu" — authentic rendering

### Idea 3 — "Sunrise Workflow" (Probability: 0.05)

**Design Movement:** Warm Minimalism — Bhakti meets Bauhaus

**Core Principles:**
1. Cream and terracotta palette — earthy, grounded, devotional
2. Strong typographic hierarchy using weight contrast (ultra-bold headers, light body)
3. Card-grid layout with generous gutters — each module is a standalone tile
4. Workflow progress shown as a horizontal timeline at the top

**Color Philosophy:** Background: warm white (#FDFAF5); Primary: deep terracotta (#B5451B); Accent: marigold yellow (#F0A500); Text: dark charcoal (#2C2416) — the palette of a morning puja

**Layout Paradigm:** Top progress bar (steps 1–11) + scrollable card grid below. Each step expands into a full-width panel when active.

**Signature Elements:**
- Terracotta geometric tiles as card backgrounds
- Marigold flower SVG motifs as decorative accents
- Bold step numbers in oversized display type

**Interaction Philosophy:** Energetic and encouraging — like a morning ritual. Each completed step animates a marigold bloom.

**Animation:** Cards scale up (1.02x) on hover. Step completion triggers a confetti burst in terracotta/marigold. Progress bar fills with a warm gradient.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Playfair Display" for step titles
- Body: "DM Sans" for content
- Telugu: "Noto Sans Telugu"
